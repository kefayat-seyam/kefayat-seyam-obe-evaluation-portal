External Faculty

Teachers from other departments who take courses for ECE.

First-year students do not only study ECE subjects. Their Mathematics, Physics and Humanities courses are taught by staff from other departments of the university. Those teachers still need a login here, because they set course outcomes and enter marks just like an ECE teacher does.

This page is where the head records them and the courses they take for us. They are ordinary faculty accounts carrying external: true and the name of their own department, which is what keeps them out of the Faculty Info list.

Name Department Designation Email Courses

External teacher model: { id, role:'faculty', external:true, externalDepartment, name, email, password, designation, departmentId }. departmentId stays ECE because that is the department they teach for; externalDepartment is where they actually work. Removing one takes them off their courses first.
