Question Setup

List the questions of each paper and say which CO and PO every question tests.

A paper's questions do not have to be four — put in as many as your question paper really has. Each question's marks are shared between one or more CO / PO pairs, and those shares are what the attainment report counts. A paper you never set up here simply keeps the old single-box mark entry.
