CO / PO Attainment

How much of your class reached each course outcome and each program outcome.

Every question you set up carries one or more CO / PO tags with a share of its marks. A student who scores, say, 60% of a question is credited with 60% of each of that question's tag marks. Adding those up per outcome gives the student a got / max figure; when their percentage reaches the course threshold they have attained that outcome. The class figure is simply the percentage of examined students who attained it.
